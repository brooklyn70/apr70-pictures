# i18n — field inventory (which strings translate, and which do not)

**Date:** 2026-09-07 · **Branch:** `i18n/groundwork` · **Migration:** `cms/src/migrations/20260907_195421_i18n_localization.ts`
**Plan:** `docs/plans/APR70_BRAND_BASELINE_AND_LAYERED_CINEMA_PLAN.md` §F.2 phase 2

Every `text` / `textarea` / `richText` field in the CMS is listed below, whether it
was marked `localized: true` or deliberately left alone. Non-text fields (uploads,
relationships, selects, checkboxes, numbers, dates, brand colour tokens) are never
localized and are not listed.

## The rule applied

Localize what a reader reads. Leave alone what identifies a thing.

- **Localized** — headings, eyebrows, kickers, ledes, body copy, captions, credits,
  quotes, attributions, footnotes, button and link labels, nav link labels, ARIA
  labels, SEO title and description, chrome strings, loglines, provenance lines,
  meta lines, synopses, and Media `alt`.
- **Not localized** — slugs, URLs, email addresses, numbers, dates in code form,
  keycodes and short codes that are the same in every language (`212`, `310`, `NRC`,
  `VOL. 01`), brand and legal entity names, performer and proper names, and
  **titles**. Titles are shared across locales: a picture called *Sea Gate* is called
  *Sea Gate* in Lisbon. This follows the Vik Muniz Studio lesson — `series_locales`
  there has no `title` column, on purpose.

## Judgment calls worth knowing about

| Field | Call | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `Project.title`, `Project.slug` | not localized | The title is the work's name; the slug is the URL. Both shared. |
| `Project.storyPlace` | localized | "Brooklyn" is prose set beside the story year, and place names do change language. `storyYear` stays shared. |
| `V9SlateListBlock.rows.title` | not localized | It is a display override for a property title — same rule as `Project.title`. |
| `V9SlateListBlock.rows.meta` | not localized | Ignored on the public site; a legacy seed field kept only for re-seed compatibility. |
| `V9DivisionStripBlock.divisions.name` | not localized | "(212) Pictures" is a division brand name. Its `blurb` is localized. |
| `SiteSettings.brandLabel`, `legalEntity` | not localized | Brand and legal entity. The `tagline` beside them is localized. |
| `SiteSettings.aiMark.text` | localized | The AI disclosure stamp is read by the viewer, so it must be readable in their language. |
| `Media.aiFrame` | not localized (and not text) | The disclosure is a fact about the picture, not about the caption's language. See below. |
| `FoundingRoll.*` | not localized | Enrollment records submitted by visitors, not site copy. |
| `PlaybillBlock.title`, `ZineMastheadBlock.title` | not localized | Episode title and masthead nameplate. |

## The AI Mark: why a new field exists

The site used to decide a frame was AI-generated by matching the English phrase
`/ai[\s-]?generated/i` in the caption or credit printed next to it. That rule dies
the first time a caption is translated — the disclosure stamp would silently
disappear, which is a compliance failure and not a cosmetic one. `Media.aiFrame` is
now the stored, authoritative, un-localized flag. `cms/scripts/backfill-ai-mark.ts`
ticked it on the 39 media documents the old regex was catching.
