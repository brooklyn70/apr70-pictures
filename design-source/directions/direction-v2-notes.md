# Direction mockups, round 2 (Wave J)

Three new theme candidates for Marco's visual pick, alongside the two survivors (Screening Room, Cutting Room). Mockups only... no build code. Every file is self-contained static HTML in this folder, uses the locked brand system (Futura Std local OTFs, Barlow, Share Tech Mono, black/white + orange #E85D04, division colors per SPEC §1), carries real site copy from `web/src/content/v4.ts` verbatim, and ships BOTH modes via the corner toggle (or `?mode=light` / `?mode=dark` in the URL). PD images referenced with credit lines; no AI people; no tickers; no purple; no glassmorphism.

## Direction F · Mission Control (`direction-f-mission-control.html`)

The mandated x.ai direction. Studied the live https://x.ai in a real browser (2026-07-06) and deployed its structural DNA through the APR 70 system: sticky hairline nav with pill CTAs, a centered hero with an eyebrow chip (the Dispatch No. 1 chip plays x.ai's beta-chip role), display type at line-height 1.0 with tight negative tracking instead of house crush, a 1280px container with a 672px reading column, a full-bleed hairline-grid stat band where the indicia become giant numerals (3 / 1 / 1), product-card doors with status dots and arrow links, a news-row slate list with pill division chips, and a sitemap footer. The one loud x.ai motif kept: the orange grain plate with corner handles, carrying the OWI rehearsal photo. Type deployment: Futura Medium 500 does the display work (calm, not Heavy), Barlow does all UI and body, mono demoted to keycodes. It borrows x.ai's whitespace discipline, one-accent restraint, and sentence-height display; it rejects x.ai's brand (no universalSans, no lowercase nameplate, the orange full stop and mono keycodes stay APR 70). Motion signature: telemetry settle... short, even, one-time staggered rises; nothing scrubs, nothing loops.

## Direction G · On Air (`direction-g-on-air.html`)

Original. The company does radio, so the front door becomes the control room: a status bar with a lit ON AIR lamp, a frequency-ruler nav with tick marks where pages are station presets and the active page gets the orange needle, the nameplate as a station ID sign (Futura Heavy with a lamp-glow full stop in dark mode), indicia as toggle-switch chips, the identity strip inside a bezeled instrument window next to the 1938 ON AIR photograph, the Troupe program as a board of stacked display words with an outlined last line, doors as three channel modules with division-color indicator lamps, and the slate as a broadcast log with glowing pips. Dark default is broadcast night (warm near-black); light is the daylight studio (warm paper, ink bezels, lamps unlit). Type deployment: Futura Heavy for station IDs and headings, Barlow body, Share Tech Mono for frequencies, timecodes, and log metadata. Motion signature: tuning in... blocks arrive from slight blur and drift to locked sharp, one time, and the only scroll-scrub is the VU meter that fills as you read the identity panel.

## Direction H · The Writers' Wall (`direction-h-writers-wall.html`)

Original. The identity copy names it: "Index cards on a wall in Long Island City." The whole front door is that wall. Every block is a pinned card: the nameplate on a big title card, indicia on a ruled red-top index card, the identity paragraph on a taped memo card with an orange marker highlight on the first sentence, Dispatch No. 1 as the one solid-orange card on the board, the Troupe program as a poster card beside a taped photo print, doors as three pinned cards (with the Abbott El photo paper-clipped to the 212 card) joined by a single sagging orange thread, and the slate as a stack of seven slips with division-color pushpins. Light is the daylight office wall; dark is the 2 a.m. wall... the room goes dark under a work lamp and the cards stay paper so the type never loses contrast. Type deployment: Futura Heavy only at title-card scale, Futura Bold + BoldOblique for card headings and taglines, Barlow body, mono as typed annotations. Texture: card stock rules, pushpins, tape, wall grain... the only tactile-collage theme on the slate. Motion signature: pinned... each card settles onto the wall once with a small drop and a relaxing tilt, and the thread draws itself; nothing loops.
