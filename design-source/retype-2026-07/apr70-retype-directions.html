The classics never go out of style

Four complete type systems, all free/open-source (SIL OFL — zero licensing exposure, self-hostable under the colophon law). Direction 1 is the continuity pick — the geometric spirit of the old face, kept. Directions 2–4 are genuinely different voices: Deco New York, trade-paper condensed, prestige classical. Every direction shows the full hierarchy, the logo system dark + light, favicons, letterhead, and card.

DIRECTION 01 · THE CONTINUITY PICK — ALREADY LIVE ON APR70.COM

Continuity — Jost

Jost (display) · Newsreader (text) · Courier Prime (mono/meta). Jost is an open-source revival of 1920s German geometric sans design — the same Bauhaus lineage as the retired face. At the weights the brand actually uses (Light/Book/Medium caps, wide-tracked), the difference is nearly invisible. The whole v10 system, the holding page, and every existing layout survive unchanged. Lowest risk, zero re-design cost; the emergency swap this morning already proved it on the live page.

DIRECTION 02 · NEW SYSTEM

Photoplay Deco — Josefin Sans

Josefin Sans (display) · Cormorant Garamond (text) · Josefin Light (meta). Geometric like the old face, but tilted twenty years earlier: 1920s Deco, elegant small x-height, vintage title-card energy. This is the Kaufman-Astoria / silent-photoplay New York — Chrysler Building lobby, not Bauhaus classroom. The Cormorand old-style serif underneath gives it a literary, program-notes warmth. Premium, austere, unmistakably cinema.

DIRECTION 03 · NEW SYSTEM

Trade Paper — Oswald

Oswald (display, condensed) · Source Serif 4 (text) · Oswald Light (meta). The other New York: the trades. Variety mastheads, one-sheet billing blocks, Alternate Gothic pressroom muscle. Condensed caps stack tall and hit hard — this is the loudest and most modern-editorial of the four, closest to A24/Neon poster energy while staying period-correct. Body text in a sturdy transitional serif keeps the broadsheet register.

DIRECTION 04 · NEW SYSTEM

Marquee Classical — Marcellus

Marcellus (display, lapidary Roman caps) · Newsreader (text) · Fraunces italic (voice/accents). The prestige register: inscriptional capitals in the Trajan tradition — the face of every studio-era epic main title — but drawn as a modern open-source Roman. Reads as the oldest and most austere of the four: carved stone, live orchestra, roadshow engagement. Fraunces italic underneath supplies the human, editorial counter-voice the caps need.
