L.A. Dolce Vita:
Seven Voices and a Clock

A radio drama for seven voices and a clock. When we cut, the clock will tell you where you stand. Listen for it.

From APR 70 Pictures, the APR 70 Troupe, performing number one of its programs: L.A. Dolce Vita, a radio drama for seven voices and a clock. One story, three places at once: a ranch in Mojave County, Arizona; the city of Los Angeles; a train crossing Italy toward Venice on the first night of Carnevale. Ten days in February. This is the first day.

A scandal has a first day. So does the girl on the motorcycle. So does the man on the train. Program No. 1 plays here. Leave your address at the masthead and the Troupe will tell you when No. 2 exists.

Voices first, film after. The Troupe works in the old tradition: a resident company, seven players and many parts to a player, and we will not tell you which. Numbered programs, performed by human actors and released as finished things. No dates, no episodes dropped into a feed. Just No. 1, then No. 2. The radio drama came before the picture once, and it can again. The Troupe will return.
