1 · Screening Room

House design + default. Dark poster modernism: Futura Heavy at poster scale, orange leader, sprocket chrome, scroll-read type, film-gate wipes.

2 · Photoplay

The picture magazine. Ivory stock (cooled per your ruling), condensed cover lines with letterpress shadow, serif text, halftone plates, No. 1 seal, spine-tab nav, plates that develop like prints.

3 · Trade Paper

The broadsheet. Cooled parchment, justified two-column text with drop caps, double rules and dotted leaders, ink-stamp folio nav. The anti-spectacle theme: rules draw themselves, nothing flies.

4 · Cutting Room

The editing bench. Black-and-white only, Share Tech Mono leads, SMPTE timecode gutters, registration-frame type specimens, grayscale plates. Motion is hard cuts: steps() easing, no dissolves. One orange grease-pencil cue per page.

5 · Picture Palace

The night marquee. Deco black-blue with brand navy, bulb-band marquee nameplate with a glowing jewel stop, vertical letter towers for the cover story, marquee-cell nav that lights on hover, letters that warm up in sequence.
