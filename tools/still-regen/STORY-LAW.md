# The Story Law (Marco, 2026-07-16)

Every generated shot must PUSH THE STORY — not just show character or location.

The test: a stranger looking at the frame must be able to tell **what is happening**
and that **something is at stake**. If the frame only answers *where* or *who*, cut it.

The consequence lives in the frame:
- Not the bridge — the two men on top of its tower after the job, cigarette lit,
  pistol still in hand, bloody tarp at their feet (`11-02-alpha-yy/01-hero/alpha-yy-hero-1.png`
  is the canonical example).
- Not the block — Housie Mousie pulling up and taking the kids on a joy ride.
- Not the hospital — Aimee at her mother's bedside after her mother hit her with the car.
- Not city hall — the mayor mid-scrum, flashbulbs firing, the front page already set.

This law composes with the Limelight standard (name the real place, own the light,
refs carry cast/wardrobe only). Limelight owns HOW the frame is made; the Story Law
owns WHY the frame exists.
