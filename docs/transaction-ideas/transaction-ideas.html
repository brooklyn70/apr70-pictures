Four ways the studio could transact

The Founding Roll is already the studio's first transaction, live and built on its own. These four are the bench: candidates the studio may test later, drawn here as direction, not product. Each panel is a picture of how the surface could look, sketched with its controls switched off. Read them the way you read storyboards, one question at a time: is this the one worth building next.

Design only. Every field, box, and button on this page is inert. No form submits, no endpoint answers, no email or dollar is taken anywhere below. Names, dates, and numbers on the mock surfaces are placeholders for the look, nothing more.

1 INT. THE DISPATCH - MORNING

The APR 70 Dispatch

The slate, in your inbox, when there is something worth sending.

A free enrollment tied to the work. When a script advances, a title goes public, or a screening is set, the dispatch carries the notice. Now and then it carries a short editorial note from the writer about what a scene is trying to do. It is one list, run by the person who writes the pages, sent only when there is real news, and open to leave at any time.

2 INT. THE EDITION - CONTINUOUS

Collector Editions

A limited artifact tied to one moment in a picture.

A frame, a script excerpt, a release milestone, issued in a fixed and numbered run. Some editions are digital, some physical, each tied to a specific project and a specific beat. These are collector media, made to be kept, and every edition states its number and its size on its face. The run closes when it closes.

3 EXT. THE BOX OFFICE - NIGHT

The Box Office

A seat held for a screening, a reading, or a preview.

Reserve a place at a live or streamed event: a screening, a table read, a first look at a cut. Early RSVP opens the room before anything is public. A held seat is a held seat, confirmed by email, with the room size stated up front so the house is honest about how many it can hold.

4 INT. THE MARQUEE - DUSK

Support Pass

Back one title, on clear terms, with a plain fence around it.

A bounded supporter tier tied to a single title or division. A supporter helps a named picture move forward and gets the maker's account of how it is going. The fence is the point: one title, one division, a stated ceiling, and a plain account of what a supporter does and does not receive. It reads as patronage of a specific work, kept well apart from anything that behaves like an investor page or a pooled ask.

Tests whether a fenced, single-title supporter tier can raise real support and stay plainly on the right side of the line, without drifting toward investor-page behavior or a pooled ask.

5 INT. THE MEMO - DAY

Pick the one or two worth a real build.

The Founding Roll stays the live first transaction; the bench waits behind it until you say go. Each panel above asks a single yes or no: is this the surface worth testing next. The design is here so the decision can be made on how it looks and feels, not on a spec. The rest is drawing.
